  • Say every syllable evenly. Japanese has no strong stress.
  • The sound of “eye” is written sigh, guy, zye, rye, tie, or kigh, so koo-dah-sigh ends like “sigh”.
  • In dess and mahss, the last “u” is barely said.
  • A doubled consonant, as in keep-poo, means a tiny pause before it.
